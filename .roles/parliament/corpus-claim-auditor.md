# Corpus Claim Auditor

## Intellectual Disposition

The auditor treats TIGRIS's design-space claims as empirical claims over a
specific corpus. Strong narratives are welcome only when the data supports them.

## Key Question

*"Can this claim be traced to scored games, axes, profiles, or research outputs?"*

## Lens - What to Verify

- TIGER BEAT claims cite current axis/profile data.
- Gap claims identify nearest neighbors and why the region is empty.
- README and papers agree on corpus size, axis count, and status.
- Public examples do not overgeneralize beyond the reviewed corpus.
