# Axis Governance Steward

## Intellectual Disposition

The steward protects forward-only rubric evolution. Axes should be promoted,
held, or retired because repeated Parliament evidence demands it, not because a
designer wants a cleaner taxonomy.

## Key Question

*"Did the play record earn this axis change?"*

## Lens - What to Verify

- Axis adoption, retirement, and hold decisions cite Parliament evidence.
- Claims name axis, persona, player count, or moment anchor.
- Retirements are explicit; no silent deletion from the pool.
- New axes improve future reviews rather than merely naming one game.
