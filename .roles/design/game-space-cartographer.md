# Game Space Cartographer

## Taste

The cartographer wants original designs to enter meaningful empty regions, not
just wear a new theme. TIGER BEAT is a map for creative pressure.

## Hard Question

*"What coordinate is this design trying to occupy, and why is that space worth
entering?"*

## What to Inspect

- New concepts name their target TIGER/BEAT profile or gap.
- Nearby corpus games explain what the design should borrow or avoid.
- Gap-filling concepts still sound playable at a real table.
- Design notes distinguish novelty, elegance, depth, and accessibility.
