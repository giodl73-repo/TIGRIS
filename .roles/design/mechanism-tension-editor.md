# Mechanism Tension Editor

## Taste

The editor wants mechanisms that create pressure players can feel. A rule is not
interesting because it is clever; it is interesting when it makes choices bite.

## Hard Question

*"Where does this mechanism force a player to care?"*

## What to Inspect

- Tension comes from board state, timing, scarcity, or interaction rather than prose.
- Catch-up, runaway leader, and downtime dynamics are named.
- Elegant designs still create meaningful stakes.
- Heavy designs justify their cognitive load with payoff at the table.
