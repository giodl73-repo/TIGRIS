# Table Experience Observer

## Audience

The observer watches whether the profile predicted by the design actually
appears in play. Scores are useful only if they explain table behavior.

## Hard Question

*"Did players experience the intended pressure, arc, interaction, and texture?"*

## What to Inspect

- Narrated playthroughs test the claimed axis profile.
- Player-lens evidence distinguishes engagement, confusion, and frustration.
- Scores are anchored to moments, not abstract designer preference.
- Amendments preserve disagreement rather than smoothing it into consensus.
