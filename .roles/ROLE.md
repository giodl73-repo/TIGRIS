# TIGRIS Role Index

TIGRIS is a board-game factory and game-design corpus. Use these roles when
changing Parliament reviews, TIGER BEAT axes, original game designs, playtest
records, research claims, or handoff state.

## Parliament

Governance roles keep the factory honest: disagreement must be well-formed,
axes must evolve from evidence, and public claims must match the corpus.

| File | Role | Primary tension |
|---|---|---|
| `parliament/axis-governance-steward.md` | Axis Governance Steward | Evidence-based rubric evolution vs. committee taste |
| `parliament/corpus-claim-auditor.md` | Corpus Claim Auditor | Strong design-space claims vs. unsupported corpus narrative |

## Design

Design roles protect board-game craft and intentional gap-filling.

| File | Role | Primary tension |
|---|---|---|
| `design/game-space-cartographer.md` | Game Space Cartographer | Designing into real gaps vs. chasing novelty labels |
| `design/mechanism-tension-editor.md` | Mechanism Tension Editor | Elegant mechanisms vs. stakes that actually bite |

## Playtest

Playtest roles protect player experience and repeated evidence.

| File | Role | Primary tension |
|---|---|---|
| `playtest/table-experience-observer.md` | Table Experience Observer | Theoretical profile fit vs. lived table behavior |

## Review order

1. Use Design roles while reviewing or creating game concepts.
2. Use Playtest roles when interpreting narrated playthroughs and player-lens evidence.
3. Use Parliament roles before axis amendments, public framework claims, or handoff commits.
